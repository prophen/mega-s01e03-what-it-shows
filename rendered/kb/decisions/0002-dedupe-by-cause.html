Why these are separate

The shared parser reads month first. Day-first tenants entering dates through app/email or booking-v2 can get the month and day swapped. For example, harbour.dental report R-41200 describes 7 April becoming 4 July. Use the tenant directory and the actual input path; the browser locale and intake tag are guesses. Both app and email can reach the same cause.

booking-v3 instead prints MM/DD/YYYY while its form reads dates in the tenant’s day-first order. That misleading instruction is a separate defect affecting alder.physio, copper.studio, and pale.harbour form reports. Fixing the shared parser alone does not correct the hint. For the same tenant, a form report and an app report can have different causes.

The five desk-flagged reports remain in the parser group and need closer investigation within that ticket. R-41202 and R-41620 describe adjacent days; R-41209 describes June-only shifts; R-41344 describes different days for Lisbon and Toronto. R-41236 says an invite arrived before the booking, without giving timestamps. A timezone, DST or date-only conversion defect is plausible, but the export does not establish a separate root cause. Request the original timestamps, timezone IDs, inputs and invite/delivery logs. Create another ticket only if new evidence establishes a distinct cause. Verify these reported symptoms when validating the parser fix.
