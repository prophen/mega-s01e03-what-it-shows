Current rule: establish the cause

Decision 0002 replaces decision 0001. The accepted original record stays unchanged as history. Subject matches suggest candidates; they do not establish duplicates.

  1. Read the body. Record product, tenant, input channel, form version, input, expected and actual dates, and timezone.
  2. Look up tenant configuration. Browser locale and intake tags are not authoritative.
  3. Merge only with evidence of the same cause. App and email can share the parser cause despite different subjects. A booking-v3 form and an app report can have different causes despite identical subjects.
  4. With unknown tenant or insufficient evidence, keep the report separate pending investigation. Two unknown causes are not one cause. Do not merge by default.
  5. Deduplicate by report ID. Preserve received and merged timestamps separately. Recompute counts and review after fixes or new evidence.

For #4471, parser and booking-v3 hint are distinct problems. Keep the five flagged parser reports visible as investigation notes; the export does not establish an independent third cause. See the incident review.
